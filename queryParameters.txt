Query parameters are key-value pairs added after a question mark ? at the end of a URL.

They are not part of the route path itself, but are used to provide extra information like filters, searches, sorting, or pagination.

Unlike route parameters which identify a specific resource, query parameters are optional and are used to modify the request for a resource.

Multiple query parameters are separated by & symbols.

Express automatically parses them and makes them available in the req.query object.
